FTA-5O

- Weekly wage payments visibly reduce every club's balance.
- Wage totals, debt, projected weekly bill, and insolvency warnings appear in finances.
- Abilities are visible on squad cards, market cards, expanded profiles, and selected match players.
- Tied player-controlled playoff matches use a playable best-of-five penalty shootout.
- Penalty shooting uses an elongated 3x3 goal target grid.
- Shooting skill affects placement accuracy; goalkeeper skill affects reading and saves.
- Best shooters take penalties first; sudden death starts when level after five each.
- Penalty goalkeeper animation has fast launch, airborne hang, and drop phases.
- Penalty scores are retained in playoff results, post-match screens, and the home bracket.
