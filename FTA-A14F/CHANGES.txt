FTA-A14F — Ability development, Sweeper Keepers, and goalkeeper squad repair

ABILITY DEVELOPMENT
- Weekly training can now develop special abilities.
- Reaching minimum attributes only makes an ability eligible; it never guarantees it.
- Position suitability is evaluated first.
- Higher relevant attributes and overall rating improve the unlock chance.
- Age, morale, training focus, and a hidden player-specific affinity influence the weekly roll.
- Outfield players can develop Ghost Run, Bullet Pass, Eagle Eye, Iron Tackle, Powerhouse, or Shadow Mark.
- Training history records newly developed abilities and Team Setup displays them.

SWEEPER KEEPER
- Added Sweeper Keeper as a goalkeeper-only ability.
- Requires strong Speed, Defense, Movement, Aerial, and overall quality before it can develop.
- Only Sweeper Keepers may leave the penalty area.
- Ordinary goalkeepers are confined to the same legal box limits for both the player and AI.
- Removed the previous player-only extra-column goalkeeper advantage.
- Sweeper Keepers receive an extended but bounded roaming zone and retain goalkeeper save behavior.

AI
- AI Sweeper Keepers rush through balls and dangerous loose balls, support possession build-up, and position higher when the tactic suits it.
- Possession, balanced build-up, counter-pressing, and quick-counter teams are more likely to train or recruit a Sweeper Keeper.
- AI transfer scoring values Sweeper Keepers according to tactical fit.

GOALKEEPER SQUAD RULE
- Every squad is normalized to exactly one goalkeeper.
- Existing saves with duplicate goalkeepers keep the best one and replace extras with outfield players.
- Reserve generation can no longer create an accidental second goalkeeper.
- Goalkeeper transfers replace/exchange the current keeper so neither club ends with zero or two goalkeepers.
