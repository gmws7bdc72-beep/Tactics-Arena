FOOTBALL TACTICS ARENA — PHASE 3 CUMULATIVE PATCH
==================================================

This ZIP includes Phases 1, 2, and 3:

PHASE 1
- Five-tab bottom menu with the blue-highlighted centre Play tab.

PHASE 2
- Full Team / Club Profiles page.

PHASE 3
- Training tab inside Team Setup.
- Persistent player potential, morale, progress, age, development history,
  season growth, total growth, and individual focus.
- Team workload: Light, Balanced, or Intense.
- Individual focuses: Balanced, Passing, Shooting, Long Balls, Defending,
  Goalkeeping, and Movement.
- Movement replaces Luck throughout the player database and match engine.
- Existing saved players using Luck are migrated automatically to Movement.
- Player training is processed after every regular-season or playoff match week.
- AI clubs train every match week and strategically choose focuses based on
  player role, squad weaknesses, club tactic, and current attributes.
- AI development is saved inside league data and affects the players used in
  matches and shown in Club Profiles.
- Player and AI development carries into new seasons. Ages increase and only
  the season-growth counters reset.

INSTALL
-------
1. Place this ZIP in the main Replit workspace — the folder that contains the
   "artifacts" directory.

2. Run:

   unzip -o FTA-Phase-3-Training-System-CUMULATIVE.zip
   cd artifacts/fta-game
   pnpm typecheck && pnpm build

3. Start the game normally.

You do not need to delete localStorage or restart your league. The patch
migrates old saved player stats and creates missing development data.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Training gains are earned after match weeks, not by repeatedly opening the
  Training tab.
- A full stat point is awarded when the player's progress meter reaches 100%.
- Players close to their potential improve more slowly.
- Intense training increases progress but applies a small morale cost.
