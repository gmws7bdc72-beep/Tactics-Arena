What changed:
- Added every legal 6v6 outfield shape with at least one defender: all D-M-F combinations totaling five outfielders.
- Team Setup now lists all 15 legal 6v6 formations.
- AI formations are now generated by mirroring the player formations, so each new player formation has a matching AI version.
- AI teams now use more distinct formations matched to their tactics.
- Player default team and two-player defaults now use the new 2-1-2 Classic Balance formation.
- Team Profiles now show the full formation name instead of only the formation id.
