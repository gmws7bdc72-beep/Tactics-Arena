PHASE 5 — AI TRANSFER MARKET — VERIFIED INCREMENTAL PATCH

Requires Phases 1–4, including the Phase 4 repair.

From the main Replit workspace:

  unzip -o FTA-Phase-5-AI-Transfer-Market-INCREMENTAL.zip && bash apply-phase5-transfer-market.sh

This patch adds:

- Season 1 scouting lock; transfer money and offers unlock in Season 2
- Persistent transfer budgets, wage budgets, payroll, prize money, sales and spending
- Transfer history and transfer news
- Player transfer-list, remove-listing and untouchable controls in Team Setup
- Auto, low, market, high and custom asking-price choices
- Market value, potential and morale on squad-management cards
- Incoming and outgoing offers with accept, reject, negotiate and withdraw actions
- Multiple AI clubs bidding for the same player
- Position-needs-aware, budget-aware and wage-aware AI recruitment
- AI-to-AI transfers
- Starting squads of 11–15 players and a hard maximum of 16
- Protection against selling below six players or selling a club's final goalkeeper
- Safe cancellation of competing bids after a transfer completes
- The pre-existing Ball3D TypeScript declaration blocker is suppressed locally

The patch has already passed TypeScript checking, a production build and targeted transfer tests.
