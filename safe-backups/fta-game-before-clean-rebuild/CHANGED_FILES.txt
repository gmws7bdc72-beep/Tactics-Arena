Added/updated features:
- Full player stat system for every player on every team.
- Team tactics programmed for every AI team.
- Tactics range from slow possession progression to direct play, pressing, low-block counters, wide crossing, quick counters, and long-ball target-man football.
- Long-ball command support: aerial passes travel above the pitch, draw differently, and cannot be intercepted like normal ground passes until the ball drops near its landing point.
- AI long-ball decision logic based on team tactic, player long passing, vision, aerial ability, pressure, and target movement.
- Team Profiles screen from the home menu to view every team, tactic, and every player's stats.
- Permanent rest-defense anchor: at least one AI defender/midfielder stays significantly behind the rest of the team.
- Saved/older player teams still work; missing new stats fall back safely in display and simulation.

Install plan:
1. Upload fta-game-upgraded.zip to the top level of Replit.
2. Run:
   cd ~/workspace
   rm -rf /tmp/fta-upgrade
   mkdir -p /tmp/fta-upgrade
   unzip -q fta-game-upgraded.zip -d /tmp/fta-upgrade
   ls /tmp/fta-upgrade/artifacts/fta-game
   rm -rf artifacts/fta-game-BACKUP
   cp -r artifacts/fta-game artifacts/fta-game-BACKUP
   rm -rf artifacts/fta-game
   cp -r /tmp/fta-upgrade/artifacts/fta-game artifacts/fta-game

Undo plan:
