FTA-A15 changes

Custom user bidding
- Keeps the Low, Ask and Strong quick-bid buttons.
- Adds a custom bid field to every eligible player card.
- Custom fees are entered in millions and rounded to the nearest $250,000.
- The exact custom amount is shown on the submit button before the bid is sent.
- Normal budget, wage, squad-size and availability validation still applies.

Custom counteroffers
- Incoming AI offers now allow any custom counteroffer amount.
- Replaces the fixed +15% negotiation button with an editable amount field.
- Counteroffers remain pending until the AI club answers during transfer processing.

AI bid selection
- AI clubs now deliberately choose their own fee instead of using one narrow random multiplier.
- Bid size responds to positional shortage, upgrade quality, tactical fit, player status and available budget.
- Desperate or perfect-fit buyers can bid strongly above asking price.
- Bargain hunters can test sellers with lower offers, especially for debt-listed players.
- AI selling clubs also choose varied counteroffer amounts based on squad depth, listing status and negotiating leverage.
