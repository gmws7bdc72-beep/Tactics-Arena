PHASE 6 — MANAGEMENT POLISH — VERIFIED INCREMENTAL PATCH

Requires the verified Phase 5 AI Transfer Market patch.

From the main Replit workspace:

  unzip -o FTA-Phase-6-Management-Polish-INCREMENTAL.zip && bash apply-phase6-management-polish.sh

This patch adds:

- A compact incoming-offer popup that dismisses itself after 2.2 seconds
- Incoming offers remain available under Market → Offers after the popup closes
- Active bids separated from a persistent completed/rejected/withdrawn offer archive
- A structured transfer-news feed with offer, negotiation, transfer, finance and system filters
- Live season spending, sales, net-spend, payroll and wage-space summaries
- Player career timelines with transfer and end-of-season milestones
- A fourth History tab in every Club Profile
- Archived club season records, honours, prize money and transfer timelines
- Full end-of-season reports with champion, standings, club finances, biggest transfer and development standout
- Automatic migration of existing Phase 5 saves to the Phase 6 data format
- Duplicate-prize/report protection if season rollover is triggered more than once

This is incremental. It does not replace the project, alter match gameplay, change deployment or touch Vite configuration.

- The original Phase 5 transfer regression suite
- Phase 6 save-migration, career-history and season-report tests
- TypeScript checking
