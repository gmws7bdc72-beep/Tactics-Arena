FTA CUMULATIVE GAMEPLAY FIX

Includes:
- A playing phase now continues until every planned player action and ball action has resolved.
- AI gets the same extended action budget, so complex runs and chained actions are not cut off.
- Full-pitch long balls animate faster and finish naturally instead of crawling and snapping.
- New saves generate new names, abilities and stat sheets for the player's squad and AI squads.
- Player names remain editable in Team Setup.
- Team-name editing is restored in Customize.
- Curves are limited by action type and relevant stat; loops, backwards hooks and impossible bends are removed.
- Goalkeeper dives are capped to a true one- or two-tile radius, including diagonals.
- Includes the earlier transfer timing, season transformation, AI curve and goalkeeper animation work.

IMPORTANT:
Randomized squads apply when no existing save is loaded. Existing saves are preserved intentionally.

INSTALL FROM WORKSPACE ROOT:
unzip -o FTA-Phase-Completion-Random-Saves-Curve-GK-FIX.zip
bash FTA-Phase-Completion-Random-Saves-Curve-GK-FIX/apply-patch.sh
